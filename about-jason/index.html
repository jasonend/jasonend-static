About Jason

I’m Jason Enderle, a Senior Solutions Architect focused on helping teams build reliable, secure, and cost‑efficient systems in the cloud. I spend my days designing architectures, reviewing trade‑offs, and working directly with engineers to turn ideas into production‑ready solutions.

I come from a hands‑on engineering background, so I don’t just draw diagrams and walk away. I dive into the details, automate the boring parts, and leave teams with patterns and tooling they can maintain long after I’m gone

This site is where I share what I’m learning in the field: real‑world architecture decisions, trade‑offs that didn’t look obvious on the whiteboard, and the tools and patterns that actually hold up in production.
